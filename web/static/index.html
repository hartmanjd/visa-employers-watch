Employer Lookup Watchlist Safety Map Industries Ask
Data: DOL OFLC H-1B (LCA) and H-2B disclosure files, FY2025, DOL Wage and Hour enforcement cases, and OSHA safety and health inspections. Every number links back to its DOL record. DOL's published debarment lists are on the Debarments page. The law firms and agents behind the applications are on the Law firms and agents page.
Use this data with AI (MCP)

The same lookups are open to AI assistants as a read-only MCP server at https://visa-employer-watch.onrender.com/mcp. No account or key is needed.

To add it in Claude:

  1. In Claude Desktop or claude.ai, open Settings > Connectors and choose Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Visa Employer Watch and paste the address above.
  3. Ask a question, such as “Which H-2B employers in Texas have the most back wages?”

Any app or agent that supports MCP can use the same address, for example ChatGPT, Cursor or VS Code; look for its setting to add an MCP server or connector. A plain-text guide for AI tools is at llms.txt.
